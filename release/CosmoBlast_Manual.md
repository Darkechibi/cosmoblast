# COSMOBLAST

### Game Program™ Instructions — Atari® 2600 Video Computer System™

*Version 1.3 · Use with Joystick Controller · 1 Player*

---

## THE MISSION

Deep in space, your laser base stands alone. Invaders close in from all four
directions — above, below, left and right. Your base cannot move. It can only
turn and fire.

Aim fast. Fire faster. Every invader that reaches the base breaks a piece of it
away. Lose all your hits and the base cracks apart in a blaze of fire.

Hold the line as long as you can.

---

## GETTING STARTED

1. Turn the console **OFF**. Insert the CosmoBlast cartridge.
2. Plug a Joystick Controller into the **LEFT** controller jack.
3. Turn the console **ON**. The **COSMO BLAST** title screen appears.
4. Push the joystick **LEFT** or **RIGHT** to choose a game (1–4). The game
   number shows in the bottom corner of the screen.
5. Press the **red button** to start.

---

## USING THE CONTROLS

**Push the joystick** in any of the four directions to fire your laser that way.
A single bright beam flashes from the center of the base to the edge of the
screen and destroys any invader in its path.

- **Each push fires a burst of 3 pulses.** To fire again, push again, or roll
  to another direction.
- **One beam at a time:** a new pulse waits for the current one to finish.
- **Red button:** starts the game. It does not fire.
- **Analog pads:** the stick must settle on one direction before the laser
  turns, so drifting or diagonal sticks won't fire the wrong way.
- **Aim color:** the base changes color to show which way it is aimed.

Diagonal shots are not possible. The laser fires straight up, down, left or
right only.

---

## THE ENEMY

**Invaders** fly in a straight line from the edge of the screen toward your
base. Only one attacks at a time, but each one you destroy brings the next a
little faster.

**Satellites** are small blocks that circle your base. They appear after
every 8th invader you destroy and orbit while invaders keep coming. Usually
one appears, sometimes two, and on rare occasions three. Each wave flies at
its own speed, and they get faster in harder games and as play speeds up.
A satellite can only be hit as it crosses one of your four firing lines, so
time your shot. They are worth big points, but don't wait too long: after 2
to 4 orbits they dive straight at your base and cost you a hit. You can still
shoot them on the way in.

---

## HITS AND THE SCORE

The number at the bottom of the screen shows two things:

```
 3  0140
 │   └──── your score
 └──────── hits remaining
```

- You begin each game with **3 hits**.
- When an invader or a diving satellite reaches the base, you lose one hit. The screen flashes red
  and the base blinks. While it blinks it cannot be hit again.
- **Extra hit:** you earn one extra hit for every **1,000 points**, with a
  chime. You can hold up to **9 hits**.
- When the last hit is gone, the base shakes, cracks and explodes, and
  **GAME OVER** appears.

### Scoring

Points depend on the game number, and they double as the invaders speed up:

| Game | Invader at start | Invader at top speed | Satellite        |
|:----:|:----------------:|:--------------------:|:----------------:|
| 1    | 1                | 2                    | 5× an invader    |
| 2    | 1                | 2                    | 5× an invader    |
| 3    | 1                | 4                    | 5× an invader    |
| 4    | 2                | 4                    | 5× an invader    |

The score tops out just below 10,000. Reaching it is the ultimate challenge.

## GAME SELECTION

| Game | Skill   | Invaders start | Speed-up                         |
|:----:|---------|----------------|----------------------------------|
| 1    | Easy    | Very slow      | Very gradual                     |
| 2    | Normal  | Slow           | Steady (the default game)        |
| 3    | Hard    | Medium         | Quick, up to double top speed    |
| 4    | Expert  | Fast           | Fastest, up to triple top speed  |

The game you choose stays selected for your next game.

## CONSOLE SWITCHES

| Switch                   | What it does                                        |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **GAME RESET**           | Restarts at the title screen at any time.           |
| **TV TYPE (Color/B&W)**  | **B&W pauses** the game. Switch back to **Color** to resume. |
| **GAME SELECT**          | Not used. Choose your game with the joystick.       |
| **DIFFICULTY switches**  | Not used.                                           |

After **GAME OVER**, wait a moment, then press the **red button** to return to
the title screen and play again.

## PLAYING ON AN EMULATOR

The cartridge file is **CosmoBlast_v1.3.a26** (a standard 4K cartridge, no
bankswitching). It runs in **Stella** and other Atari 2600 emulators, and on
flash cartridges such as Harmony, UnoCart and PlusCart for real consoles.

Stella's default keys:

| Action            | Key                                  |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Joystick          | Arrow keys (or a USB/Xbox gamepad)   |
| Red button        | Space                                |
| Game Reset        | F2                                   |
| Pause / Resume    | F4 (B&W) / F3 (Color)                |

## STRATEGY TIPS

- **Watch all four sides.** An invader can come from any direction. Keep your
  eyes on the center and react outward.
- **Roll, don't jump.** Rolling the stick between directions is faster than
  returning to center first.
- **Time the satellites.** They cross your firing lines four times per orbit.
  Push just before one reaches a line so your burst meets it.
- **Don't panic after a hit.** The base blinks for a moment and cannot be hit
  again. Use that time to line up the next shot.
- **Higher games pay more.** Game 4 starts at double Game 1's points,
  if you can survive it.
- **Game frozen?** If the picture stops and the sound cuts out, the game is
  paused by the B&W switch. Switch back to Color (F3 in Stella).
- **Analog pad drifting?** Raise the joystick deadzone in your emulator's
  input settings.
